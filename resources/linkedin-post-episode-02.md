# LinkedIn Post: Episode 2 - The Evolution of AI

History was never my favourite topic. But it becomes interesting when it explains something we use almost every day.

In Episode 02 of Namaste AI, I went back to where AI started.

It began with a simple question from Alan Turing:

"Can machines think?"

That question led to decades of experiments, big expectations, failures, AI winters, and then the breakthroughs we see around us today.

One thing that really stayed with me: AI did not suddenly appear with ChatGPT.

There were rule-based systems.
Then machines started learning patterns from examples.
Deep learning pushed image, speech, and language systems forward.
And Transformers changed how AI works with language.

Now we are seeing AI move beyond answering questions towards helping with tasks and workflows.

It is easy to get excited about the latest tool. But knowing the journey behind it makes the present moment feel more meaningful.

Shoutout to Akshay Saini for making the history of AI feel connected to what is happening today.
