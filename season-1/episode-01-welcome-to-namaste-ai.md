## Prerequisites: What You Actually Need

### You DON'T need:
- Machine Learning background
- Advanced Python
- Statistics or Calculus
- Linear Algebra
- Advanced Mathematics
- Neural Networks knowledge

### Good to have:
- Basic programming (Python or JavaScript)
- Understanding of how web applications work
- Familiarity with REST APIs (GET, POST methods)
- Basic knowledge of Node.js, Express, or React (optional)

### Minimum requirement:
> If you know **basics of programming** and **how software works**, you're ready.

Even if you're from a non-technical background (product manager, designer, etc.), you can follow along—concepts are taught from fundamentals.

## Course Philosophy: Learning That Sticks

This course balances two things:

| Depth | Engagement |
|-------|-----------|
| Deep enough to build real understanding | Fun and interesting (not boring) |
| Cover important concepts thoroughly | Accessible to all levels |
| Foundations very clear in your head | Make learning enjoyable |

The goal: Go slow, absorb concepts, enjoy the journey.

## How to Get the Most Out of This Course

### 1. Make Your Own Notes (Critical)

**Why:**
- Everyone's notes look different based on their understanding
- Your notes help YOU revise and retain concepts
- Handwriting is better for retention (but digital is okay too)

**How:**
- Use a notebook and pen (preferred for retention)
- Or use digital notes (iPad, Notion, etc.)
- Don't copy-paste these course notes—rephrase in your own words
- This takes extra time but pays off during revision

**Key insight:** Your notes ≠ instructor's notes. That's the point.

---

### 2. Do Assignments Seriously

- Assignments aren't optional—they're part of your learning
- Complete them with full effort
- They help you understand concepts better
- People often skip assignments but shouldn't

---

### 3. Join the Community

**Why it matters:**
- This journey takes 2-4 months or more if done properly
- Learning alone gets tough
- Community provides peer support, collaboration, problem-solving

**What to do:**
- Join the Namaste AI community channel
- Engage with other learners
- Help others, ask questions
- Share your progress
- All course updates and important notifications happen here

---

### 4. Be Active on Social Media

**Platforms:** LinkedIn and Twitter (most important)

**Why:**
- Showcase your learning journey to recruiters and peers
- Opens job opportunities
- Recruiters actively look at profiles and posts

**How:**
- Share your progress (e.g., "Day 1 of Namaste AI", "100-day challenge")
- Post your learning summaries or key insights
- Tag #NamasteAI or similar
- Don't need to be an influencer—just share what you learn

**Real impact:** Jobs and opportunities come through demonstrated learning, not job applications alone.

---

### 5. Give Time and Full Attention

**The most important tip:**

| Don't | Do |
|------|-----|
| Binge-watch videos | Watch slowly, pause after each video |
| Rush through content | Spend time absorbing concepts |
| Watch once and move on | Rewatch if needed (3-5 times okay) |
| Passive watching | Active engagement with exercises |
| Skip instructions | Pause and DO when asked |

**Why:**
- These are knowledge-heavy sessions
- Some concepts need multiple exposures to click
- Time invested = better engineer
- Undivided attention is essential

## Learning Tips: How to Actually Absorb Content

### Before you start
- Get a notebook or open your digital notes app
- Plan to spend 2-4+ months on this journey
- Clear your mind of expectations—focus on learning

### During each session
1. **Pause frequently** — After each video, think about what you learned
2. **Take notes** — Rephrase concepts in your own words
3. **Do what's asked** — When told to pause and write code, DO IT immediately
4. **Rewatch if needed** — Some topics might need 2-3 rewatches
5. **Reflect** — Think about why concepts matter, not just how they work

### After each session
- Review your notes within 24 hours
- Discuss with community members
- Share one key insight on social media
- Don't move to next topic until you understand current one

### Watch order
- Watch sequentially (don't skip or jump ahead)
- Everything is carefully structured
- Follow the order, even if topics feel familiar

## What NOT to Do

**These hurt your learning:**

- ❌ Watch everything in one sitting (binge-watching)
- ❌ Skip assignments or practice exercises
- ❌ Jump to later videos before finishing prerequisites
- ❌ Try to outsmart the teaching methodology
- ❌ Rely only on these notes (need the videos)
- ❌ Not pause and do exercises when instructed
- ❌ Go in unprepared (missing community, notes setup)
